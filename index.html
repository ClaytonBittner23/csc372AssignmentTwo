Make Memories at Yolo University.

It is important that during your college life, you take a moment to enjoy the world around you. We know that this time can be very stressful, and a moment to spare is a rare luxury. But one day you will look back at these times, and you will reflect on what you did. Would you rather look back and view 4 years of stress? Or 4 years of stress with a few shining golden memories? So take a moment and smell the roses. After all, you only live once!
