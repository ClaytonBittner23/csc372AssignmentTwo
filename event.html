Yolo Talent Show

Every September, Yolo University hosts its world famous talent show. There are no limits to what will be on stage! No seriously. One kid swallowed a ladder.

  • Category: Entertainment
  • Date: September 24th 2026
  • Location: The Yolo Center

    • Watch and see as Yolos students blow your mind!

    Here at Yolo University, we know that you only live once. And that is why we want to give our students and staff a chance to show the world what makes them special. Every year, we host a talent show that is open to the public. Bring your friends, or plan to make new ones. This is just one of Yolo Universitys social events. We hope to see you there!

    What to Expect

    • Be ready to be entertained
    • Performances from staff and students
    • Local food trucks will be outside, so feel free to grab dinner
    • Expect the unexpected!

    Schedule

    • 6:00PM: Food trucks arrive and start serving
    • 6:30PM: Seating is opened, preshow starts
    • 7:00PM: Main event starts
    • 9:00PM: Awards are handed out

    Participation

    Participation is strictly limited to our staff and students currently enrolled or employed at Yolo University. We understand that everyone wants to be apart of the action. And we know each and every person is special. But this is our way of giving back to our hardworking students and staff, and giving them a moment all to themselves to show the world what makes them shine. We look forward to future Yolo students going on stage one day.
